# Changelog

Lumen's own history starts here. The history of the dx.bi code it's built on is in [docs/dxbi/CHANGELOG.md](docs/dxbi/CHANGELOG.md).

## 2.30.25

- Lumen for Android phones and the Pixel Fold (`lumen-phone.apk`): the iPad app's Home, Discover, Search, Library, title pages, profiles and taste setup, a bottom bar on phones and a side rail on the opened Fold. It updates itself from these releases.
- Its player works like the iPad's: brightness and volume slides, double-tap to skip, scrub previews, picture-in-picture, per-show audio and subtitles, and a stream the phone can decode (Pixels have no Dolby Vision).
- Downloads offer High, Medium and Small at Netflix-like sizes, and a Networks page shows what Lumen has learned about each connection.
- Lumen TV: best-quality stream pick, Home and Search fixes.

## 2.30.24

- Lumen is its own app: its own name in everything it says, its own signing key, and its own releases (`lumen-tv.apk`) that the TV app offers by itself.
- TV genre pages load a row at a time and no longer bounce when you move around them.
- Apple TV-style Home on iPad: a swiping billboard, timer dots, suggestions and a profile ring.
