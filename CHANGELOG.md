# Changelog

Lumen's own history starts here. The history of the dx.bi code it's built on is in [docs/dxbi/CHANGELOG.md](docs/dxbi/CHANGELOG.md).

## 2.30.27

- Lumen TV, the Apple TV app's look: a title's picture blurs into the page as you move down, the film's name centred over the rows, About in a frosted box with Information and Languages under it, and the facts in Apple TV's order with drawn Rotten Tomatoes, IMDb and Metacritic marks.
- Lumen TV: the poster you're on grows down with what it is ("TV Show · Comedy"); watched ticks on every card; billboard dots that fill as each title's time runs.
- Lumen TV: Genius Browse on Home (moods such as Mind-Bending Mysteries and Charming Romcoms), and Search as Apple TV's, with its own letters, suggestions that preview their results, and Top Results / TV Shows / Movies.
- Lumen TV, iPad and iPhone: forced subtitles show for the lines not in English, and going back a few seconds shows subtitles over that bit in case a line was missed.
- Age ratings, languages and the cast's characters and pages work without a TMDB key of your own.
- Fixes: trailers no longer show Home behind them or play over the rows, Search keeps the remote after the keyboard closes, cast photos load again, the Christmas page no longer bounces, and a film in cinemas keeps its In Cinemas tag.

## 2.30.26

- Suggestions lean to the kind you prefer: Tune Suggestions asks "Suggest mostly" Films, A Mix or TV Shows, and Top Picks and Because you watched follow it. Before, a few long series filled every suggestion.
- Titles you rate in Rate Movies & Series count as taste even if you never played them here, and are never suggested back. The AI picks are sent your ratings with names, and look for the same director, writer and tone, plus a few less obvious finds.
- Discover has Franchises (with how far through each you are, and Complete the Collection for the next film), and Directors & Writers with a page of what each made. A TMDB key in Lumen settings fills these in fully.
- Lumen TV and phone: HDR badge, title page and player fixes.

## 2.30.25

- Lumen for Android phones and the Pixel Fold (`lumen-phone.apk`): the iPad app's Home, Discover, Search, Library, title pages, profiles and taste setup, a bottom bar on phones and a side rail on the opened Fold. It updates itself from these releases.
- Its player works like the iPad's: brightness and volume slides, double-tap to skip, scrub previews, picture-in-picture, per-show audio and subtitles, and a stream the phone can decode (Pixels have no Dolby Vision).
- Downloads offer High, Medium and Small at Netflix-like sizes, and a Networks page shows what Lumen has learned about each connection.
- Lumen TV: best-quality stream pick, Home and Search fixes.

## 2.30.24

- Lumen is its own app: its own name in everything it says, its own signing key, and its own releases (`lumen-tv.apk`) that the TV app offers by itself.
- TV genre pages load a row at a time and no longer bounce when you move around them.
- Apple TV-style Home on iPad: a swiping billboard, timer dots, suggestions and a profile ring.
