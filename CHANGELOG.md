# Changelog

Lumen's own history starts here. The history of the dx.bi code it's built on is in [docs/dxbi/CHANGELOG.md](docs/dxbi/CHANGELOG.md).

## 2.30.23

- Lumen is its own app: its own name in everything it says, its own signing key, and its own releases (`lumen-tv.apk`) that the TV app offers by itself.
- TV genre pages load a row at a time and no longer bounce when you move around them.
- Apple TV-style Home on iPad: a swiping billboard, timer dots, suggestions and a profile ring.
