Sports on Lumen TV

Mock-ups of the sports section: a sport picker made of large glass tiles, Apple Sports-style match panels in each team's colours, and a Formula 1 page laid out like GridBox (next race, standings side by side, calendar). Teams, scores and times are example data.

1. Sports home: pick a sport, see its panels

The picker replaces the old tab row. Favourite sports (★) come first and are the only tiles you see until you open "All sports". Your favourite teams are marked ★ in each sport's list and float to the top. Focus is the white fill, as everywhere else in Lumen.

4. Choosing favourites
